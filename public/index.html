How it works

Every scroll teaches you something real.

01

Tap any word. Instantly.

Subtitles under every clip are fully tappable — get the reading, romaji, and meaning of any word the second you hear it, without leaving the video.

02

Spaced review, done right.

Words you save get scheduled automatically — the ones you're about to forget come back sooner, the ones you know well come back less. No manual deck-building.

03

Every clip, matched to you.

Pick your JLPT level — N5 through N1 — and the feed personalizes itself. Beginners never get thrown into native-speed news; advanced learners never get baby talk.

04

Streaks that actually stick.

A daily streak, a heatmap of your activity, and unlockable achievements — the same habit loops that make short video addictive, pointed at something worth remembering.

05

Unlock as you learn.

Badges and milestones for the things that actually mark progress — words saved, clips watched, streaks kept — so growth feels like something, not just a number.
