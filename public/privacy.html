Privacy at Gomu

Gomu is a Japanese-learning app built around short video clips, saved vocabulary, and spaced-repetition review. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how to make us delete it.

01What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never stored by us in plain text.

If you choose Sign in with Google, we don't receive a password. Google confirms who you are and shares your name, email address, profile photo link, and Google account ID with us, which we use only to create and sign in to your Gomu account. We don't get access to anything else in your Google account.

As you use Gomu, we store the data needed to make the app work:

  • Onboarding answers — your JLPT level, why you're learning, the challenges you picked, and your daily time goal.
  • Saved vocabulary — words you tap and save from clips, and your review history for each one.
  • Watch history — which clips you've watched, so we don't repeat them and can compute your stats.
  • Daily activity — the dates you used the app, used to calculate your streak.
  • Achievements — badges you've unlocked.
  • Settings — in-app preferences such as playback and notification settings.
  • Feedback — any message you submit through the feedback form in your profile.

We also collect some data automatically:

  • Usage and diagnostics — which screens you open and what you tap, plus recordings of app sessions, so we can see where the app is confusing or broken. Every text field (such as your email, password and feedback) is hidden in these recordings. Device details such as model, operating system and app version come along with them.
  • Ad measurement events — when you install and open the app, how far you get through onboarding, and whether you sign up, start a trial or subscribe. These are linked to a random Gomu account ID, never your name or email.
  • Advertising ID — your device's advertising identifier, used to tell whether you arrived from one of our ads. On iOS we only access it if you allow tracking when asked; on Android you can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data — Gomu doesn't request access to any of these.

02How we use it

We use your data to run and improve Gomu: personalizing your feed by JLPT level, scheduling your vocabulary reviews, tracking streaks and achievements, keeping your account signed in and synced, handling your subscription, reading feedback you send us, and understanding how people use the app so we can fix problems.

We advertise Gomu on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. To know which ads work, we report installs and key in-app steps (finishing onboarding, signing up, starting a trial, subscribing) back to Meta and TikTok. This measures our own ad campaigns.

No ads inside Gomu No data sold No name or email shared with ad networks

Gomu shows no ads in the app. On iOS you can decline tracking when the app asks, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; ad measurement then relies only on Apple's privacy-preserving reports. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

03Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. It's only shared with the service providers below, for the purposes described:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Google — only if you use Sign in with Google, to verify your identity when you sign in.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play. Payments themselves are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • PostHog — product analytics and session recordings (with text fields hidden), to understand how the app is used and fix problems.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and TikTok — receive the ad measurement events and advertising ID described above, to measure our ad campaigns. Their use of this data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Gomu or our users.

04YouTube playback

Clips in Gomu stream directly from YouTube using YouTube's own embedded player. When you play a clip, YouTube (Google) may collect data about that playback — such as your IP address and device information — under its own privacy practices, separate from ours.

See Google's Privacy Policy for how YouTube handles this. Every YouTube-sourced clip in Gomu credits its channel and links back to the original video.

05Retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Danger Zone → Delete account — this removes your saved words, progress, streaks, achievements, feedback, and your login itself. It can't be undone.

No support ticket needed. Account deletion is a single in-app action, and it removes the underlying login, not just your app data. If you signed in with Google, Gomu is also disconnected from your Google account.

Don't have the app anymore? You can also request deletion by email — see Delete your Gomu account. We complete email requests within 30 days. We don't keep any account data after deletion; copies in our hosting provider's routine backups are permanently removed within 30 days.

06Security

All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted by row-level security policies scoped to your account, so one user's data isn't reachable by another.

07Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access your data — most of it is visible directly in the app (Profile, Vocab, review history).
  • Correct your onboarding answers and settings from within the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data in-app, or by emailing us — see how to delete your account.

If you're in a region with additional statutory rights (such as the EU/UK or California), those rights apply to you as provided by law, and you can reach us at the email below to exercise them.

08Children

Gomu is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

09Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

10Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request about your data? Email anvelopers368@gmail.com.
