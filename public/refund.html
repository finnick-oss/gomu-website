Refund Policy

This Refund Policy (the "Policy") sets out the terms and conditions on which Gomu ("Gomu", "we", "us" or "our") will refund payments made for a Gomu Premium subscription, including the terms of the commitment displayed in the Gomu application as "We promise: see progress or 100% money-back" (the "Progress Promise"). By purchasing a Gomu Premium subscription, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy.

01Definitions

1.1 In this Policy, unless the context requires otherwise: "Account" means the Gomu user account, identified by its email address, to which a Subscription is linked; "Application" means the official Gomu mobile application as distributed by us; "Subscription" means a Gomu Premium subscription purchased within the Application on any plan offered by us from time to time, including any plan that begins with a free trial and any plan offered at a discounted or introductory price; "Eligible Subscription" means a Subscription that satisfies every condition in section 2; "Commencement Date" means the calendar day on which the Eligible Subscription first became active, as recorded by us, whether that day fell within a free trial or a paid period; "Day" means a calendar day in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), being, for users in India, the period from 5:30 a.m. Indian Standard Time on one date to 5:29:59 a.m. Indian Standard Time on the following date; "Practice Period" means the thirty (30) consecutive Days beginning with and including the Commencement Date, such that Day 1 is the Commencement Date and Day 30 is the twenty-ninth Day after it; "Watch Time" means the time during which video clips are actively playing within the Application while you are signed in to your Account, as measured by the Application and stored on our servers; "Qualifying Day" means a Day of the Practice Period on which at least five (5) minutes (three hundred (300) seconds) of Watch Time are recorded against your Account; "Practice Requirement" means the requirement set out in section 3.1; "First Payment" means the first amount actually charged to you for the Eligible Subscription; and "Request Window" means the period beginning immediately after the end of Day 30 and ending at the end of the fourteenth (14th) Day after Day 30.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa. "Including" means "including without limitation".

02Scope and eligibility

2.1 The Progress Promise applies only to a Subscription that meets all of the following conditions: (a) the Subscription was purchased within the Application through Google Play; (b) the Commencement Date falls on or after the effective date of this Policy shown at the top of this page, and any Subscription whose Commencement Date falls before that date was sold without the Progress Promise and is not covered by it; (c) the Subscription is the first Gomu Premium subscription ever held by you, by your Account, by the Google account used to make the purchase and by the payment method used to pay for it; and (d) throughout the Practice Period you were signed in to your Account in the Application, such that Watch Time could be recorded against your Account.

2.2 Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store, where offered, are billed and refunded by Apple under Apple's own policies, and we are unable to issue refunds for them. The Progress Promise does not apply to them.

2.3 The Progress Promise may be used no more than once per natural person, once per Account, once per Google account and once per payment method, whichever limit is reached first.

03Qualifying practice requirement

3.1 To be entitled to a refund under the Progress Promise, every one of the thirty (30) Days of the Practice Period must be a Qualifying Day, meaning that at least five (5) minutes of Watch Time must be recorded against your Account on each and every Day from Day 1 to Day 30 inclusive. If any single Day of the Practice Period is not a Qualifying Day, the Practice Requirement is not met and no refund is payable under the Progress Promise.

3.2 The Practice Period begins on the Commencement Date and does not wait for the end of any free trial or for the First Payment. The Commencement Date is fixed when the Eligible Subscription first becomes active and does not change if a free trial converts into a paid period, if the Subscription renews, if the plan changes or if the Subscription is paused, cancelled or restored.

3.3 Only Watch Time counts towards the Practice Requirement. Time spent saving words, reviewing flashcards, using the dictionary, completing quizzes or otherwise having the Application open without a clip actively playing does not count towards the five (5) minutes. Watch Time recorded on one Day cannot be carried forward or backward to any other Day, so that Watch Time in excess of five (5) minutes on a Day does not make up for a shortfall on another Day.

3.4 Watch Time is recorded only while your device is connected to the internet and the Application is able to transmit it to our servers. Watch Time that is not received by our servers for any reason, including loss of connectivity, device or network faults, use of the Application while signed out, or use of an unofficial or modified version of the Application, will not be counted. Our server records of Watch Time are the sole and conclusive basis for determining whether a Day is a Qualifying Day, save in the case of manifest error on our part.

3.5 Watch Time must result from your own genuine use of the Application by actually watching clips. Watch Time generated by automated means, scripts, emulators used to simulate use, unattended or looped playback, modification of the Application, interference with the data the Application sends, or any other artificial means does not count and may result in disqualification under section 8.

3.6 Whether you have seen progress in your Japanese is a matter for your own judgement. We do not require you to demonstrate a lack of progress. The Practice Requirement, and the other conditions of this Policy, are the only conditions we will assess.

04Refund request procedure

4.1 Refunds under the Progress Promise are not issued automatically. To claim a refund, you must send a request by email to anvelopers368@gmail.com that is received by us within the Request Window, that is, after the end of Day 30 and no later than the end of the fourteenth (14th) Day after Day 30. A request received before the end of Day 30 will be treated as received at the start of the Request Window. A request received after the end of the Request Window will not be accepted.

4.2 Your request must state that you are requesting a refund under the Progress Promise and must include (a) the email address of your Account, and should be sent from that email address where possible, and (b) the Google Play order number for the First Payment, which begins with "GPA." and appears on the receipt sent to you by Google Play and in the Payments & subscriptions section of Google Play. You may, but are not required to, tell us why you did not see progress.

4.3 If we cannot match your request to an Eligible Subscription, we may ask you for further information. If you do not provide the information requested within fourteen (14) days of our request, we may close your request without a refund.

05Verification and determination

5.1 On receiving a valid request within the Request Window, we will review your Subscription, your Account and the Watch Time recorded for each Day of the Practice Period, and will notify you of our decision by email, normally within seven (7) business days.

5.2 If all conditions of this Policy are met, we will issue the refund described in section 6. If any condition is not met, we will tell you which condition was not met, including, where applicable, the Days of the Practice Period that were not Qualifying Days.

5.3 Our determination is made on the basis of the records described in section 3.4. Nothing in this section limits any right you have under applicable law to dispute a determination.

06Refund amount and method

6.1 A refund under the Progress Promise is one hundred per cent (100%) of the First Payment, being the amount you were actually charged for the Eligible Subscription, after any discount or introductory price and inclusive of any taxes collected by Google Play on that payment.

6.2 Only the First Payment is refundable under the Progress Promise. Renewal payments, payments for any later Subscription and any other amounts are not refundable under the Progress Promise, including any renewal that is charged before a refund of the First Payment is processed. You may cancel your Subscription at any time to prevent future renewals.

6.3 If your Subscription began with a free trial and you cancelled before the end of the free trial, no payment was taken and there is nothing to refund.

6.4 Refunds are issued only through Google Play, to the payment method used for the First Payment. We do not issue refunds in cash, by bank transfer, as store credit or as gift cards. The time taken for a refund to appear depends on Google Play and on your bank or payment provider and is outside our control.

07Consequences of a refund

7.1 When the First Payment is refunded, the Subscription to which it relates ends and access to Premium features is removed from your Account. Your saved words, review history and other learning data are not deleted by reason of the refund and remain in your Account unless you delete your Account.

7.2 Following a refund under the Progress Promise, no later Subscription held by you, your Account, the Google account or the payment method concerned will be covered by the Progress Promise, and we may decline to offer you free trials, discounts or introductory prices in future.

08Exclusions and disqualification

8.1 No refund is payable under the Progress Promise where: (a) the Practice Requirement in section 3.1 has not been met on every Day of the Practice Period; (b) the request is not received within the Request Window; (c) the Subscription does not meet every condition in section 2, including where it was not purchased within the Application through Google Play, began before the effective date of this Policy, or is not the first Gomu Premium subscription held by you, your Account, your Google account or your payment method; (d) the Progress Promise has already been used by you, your Account, your Google account or your payment method; (e) any Watch Time relied on was generated in breach of section 3.5; (f) the First Payment has already been refunded, charged back or reversed by Google Play, your bank or your payment provider, or a dispute or chargeback is pending; (g) you deleted your Account before we completed our review, as deletion permanently removes the records needed to verify the Practice Requirement; or (h) the Subscription was obtained or used in breach of our terms, or the request forms part of an attempt to obtain more than one refund, including by using multiple Accounts, Google accounts or payment methods.

8.2 Where we reasonably believe that section 8.1(e) or 8.1(h) applies, we may refuse the request and may suspend the Account concerned, without prejudice to any other right we have.

09Cancellation, third-party refunds and statutory rights

9.1 You may cancel your Subscription at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. On cancellation, you will retain access to Premium features until the end of the period for which you have already paid and will not be charged again. Cancellation does not of itself entitle you to a refund of any amount already paid, save as provided in this Policy or required by law.

9.2 Google Play operates its own refund policy for purchases made through it, under which you may in certain circumstances request a refund directly from Google. Any refund granted by Google is governed by Google's policy, and a payment refunded by Google cannot be refunded again under the Progress Promise.

9.3 Nothing in this Policy excludes or limits any right or remedy you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you, including, in India, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the rules made under it. Where such law gives you a right to a refund in circumstances not described in this Policy, that right is unaffected.

10Amendments

10.1 We may amend this Policy from time to time, including by changing the conditions of the Progress Promise for new Subscriptions. The amended Policy takes effect from the effective date shown at the top of this page. An amendment will not reduce the rights that applied to an Eligible Subscription on its Commencement Date, and each Eligible Subscription remains governed by the version of this Policy in force on its Commencement Date.

11General

11.1 If any provision of this Policy is held to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. Our failure to enforce any provision of this Policy shall not constitute a waiver of that provision. This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle the data, including Watch Time, used to verify the Practice Requirement.

12Contact

12.1 Refund requests under section 4, and any question about this Policy, should be sent to anvelopers368@gmail.com.
