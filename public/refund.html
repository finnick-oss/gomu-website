Refund Policy

This Refund Policy (the "Policy") sets out the terms and conditions on which Gomu ("Gomu", "we", "us" or "our") will refund payments made for a Gomu Premium subscription, including the terms of the commitment displayed in the Gomu application as "7-day money-back guarantee" and "Try it risk-free" (the "7-Day Money-Back Guarantee"), which is offered only on plans of six (6) months or longer. In short: watch clips in the app for at least fifteen (15) minutes on each of the seven (7) days starting on the day of your first payment, then ask for your refund within the following week. Renewals are not covered. By purchasing a Gomu Premium subscription, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy.

01Definitions

1.1 In this Policy, unless the context requires otherwise: "Account" means the Gomu user account, identified by its email address, to which a Subscription is linked; "Application" means the official Gomu mobile application as distributed by us; "Subscription" means a Gomu Premium subscription purchased within the Application on any plan offered by us from time to time, including any plan that begins with a free trial and any plan offered at a discounted or introductory price; "Payment" means an amount actually charged to you for a Subscription, including the first amount charged when a free trial ends and any renewal; "First Payment" means the first Payment charged for a Subscription, being the amount charged when you purchase it or, where it begins with a free trial, the amount charged when the free trial ends, and not any renewal or later Payment; "Day" means a calendar day in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), being, for users in India, the period from 5:30 a.m. Indian Standard Time on one date to 5:29:59 a.m. Indian Standard Time on the following date; "Practice Days" means the seven (7) consecutive Days beginning with and including the Day on which the First Payment was charged, such that Day 1 is the Day of the First Payment and Day 7 is the sixth Day after it; "Watch Time" means the time during which video clips are actively playing within the Application while you are signed in to your Account, as measured by the Application and stored on our servers; "Qualifying Day" means a Day on which at least fifteen (15) minutes (nine hundred (900) seconds) of Watch Time are recorded against your Account; and "Request Window" means the period beginning immediately after the end of Day 7 and ending at the end of Day 14, being the thirteenth Day after the Day of the First Payment.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation. Words in the singular include the plural and vice versa. "Including" means "including without limitation".

02Scope and eligibility

2.1 The 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee applies only to a Subscription on a plan with a billing period of six (6) months or longer, such as the 6-month plan, purchased within the Application through Google Play while the Application displayed the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee for that plan. It does not apply to weekly, monthly or other plans with a billing period shorter than six (6) months, and the Application does not display it for them.

2.2 Under the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee, you may obtain a full refund of the First Payment for that Subscription if (a) every one of the seven (7) Practice Days is a Qualifying Day, as set out in section 3, and (b) your request is received within the Request Window, as set out in section 4. Only the First Payment is covered: renewals and any later Payments are not refundable under the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee. You do not need to explain why you are requesting a refund.

2.3 If your Subscription began with a free trial, nothing is charged during the free trial and there is nothing to refund. The Practice Days run from the First Payment, charged when the free trial ends. If you cancel before the end of the free trial, you will not be charged.

2.4 Throughout the Practice Days you must be signed in to your Account in the Application, so that Watch Time can be recorded against your Account.

2.5 Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store, where offered, are billed and refunded by Apple under Apple's own policies, and we are unable to issue refunds for them. The 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee does not apply to them.

03Daily practice requirement

3.1 To be entitled to a refund, every one of the seven (7) Practice Days must be a Qualifying Day, meaning that at least fifteen (15) minutes of Watch Time must be recorded against your Account on each and every Day from Day 1 to Day 7 inclusive. If any single Practice Day is not a Qualifying Day, no refund is payable under the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

3.2 Only Watch Time counts. Time spent saving words, reviewing flashcards, using the dictionary, completing quizzes or otherwise having the Application open without a clip actively playing does not count towards the fifteen (15) minutes. Watch Time recorded on one Day cannot be carried forward or backward to any other Day, so that Watch Time in excess of fifteen (15) minutes on a Day does not make up for a shortfall on another Day.

3.3 Watch Time is recorded only while your device is connected to the internet and the Application is able to transmit it to our servers. Watch Time that is not received by our servers for any reason, including loss of connectivity, device or network faults, use of the Application while signed out, or use of an unofficial or modified version of the Application, will not be counted. Our server records of Watch Time are the sole and conclusive basis for determining whether a Day is a Qualifying Day, save in the case of manifest error on our part.

3.4 Watch Time must result from your own genuine use of the Application by actually watching clips. Watch Time generated by automated means, scripts, emulators used to simulate use, unattended or looped playback, modification of the Application, interference with the data the Application sends, or any other artificial means does not count and may result in disqualification under section 7.

04Refund request procedure

4.1 Refunds are not issued automatically. Your request must be received within the Request Window, that is, after the end of Day 7 and no later than the end of Day 14. A request received before the end of Day 7 will be treated as received at the start of the Request Window. A request received after the end of the Request Window will not be accepted.

4.2 You may request a refund in the Application, while signed in to your Account, by tapping Profile, then Settings, then Contact support, choosing "Refund" and sending your request, or by email to anvelopers368@gmail.com. A request made in the Application is linked to your Account automatically; we may ask you for the Google Play order number of the First Payment if we need it to find it. An email request should be sent from the email address of your Account where possible and must include (a) the email address of your Account and (b) the Google Play order number of the First Payment, which begins with "GPA." and appears on the receipt sent to you by Google Play and in the Payments & subscriptions section of Google Play.

4.3 If we cannot match a request to a Payment, we may ask you for further information. If you do not provide the information requested within fourteen (14) days of our request, we may close your request without a refund.

05Verification and determination

5.1 On receiving a valid request, we will review your Subscription, your Account and the Watch Time recorded for each of the Practice Days, and will notify you of our decision by email, normally within three (3) working days of the later of our receiving your request and the start of the Request Window.

5.2 If all conditions of this Policy are met, we will issue the refund described in section 6. If any condition is not met, we will tell you which condition was not met, including, where applicable, the Practice Days that were not Qualifying Days.

5.3 Our determination is made on the basis of the records described in section 3.3. Nothing in this section limits any right you have under applicable law to dispute a determination.

06Refund amount and method

6.1 A refund under the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee is one hundred per cent (100%) of the First Payment, being the amount you were actually charged, after any discount or introductory price and inclusive of any taxes collected by Google Play on the First Payment.

6.2 Refunds are issued only through Google Play, to the payment method used for the First Payment. Google Play notifies you by email when the refund is made. We do not issue refunds in cash, by bank transfer, as store credit or as gift cards. The time taken for a refund to appear depends on Google Play and on your bank or payment provider and is outside our control.

6.3 When the First Payment is refunded, the Subscription to which it relates ends and access to Premium features is removed from your Account. Your saved words, review history and other learning data are not deleted by reason of the refund and remain in your Account unless you delete your Account. Renewals and any later Payments are not covered by the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee, including a renewal charged before a refund of the First Payment is processed. You may cancel your Subscription at any time to prevent future renewals.

07Exclusions and disqualification

7.1 No refund is payable under the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee where: (a) any of the seven (7) Practice Days is not a Qualifying Day; (b) the request is not received within the Request Window; (c) the Subscription was not purchased within the Application through Google Play, is on a plan with a billing period shorter than six (6) months, or was purchased while the Application did not display the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee; (d) any Watch Time relied on was generated in breach of section 3.4; (e) the First Payment has already been refunded, charged back or reversed by Google Play, your bank or your payment provider, or a dispute or chargeback is pending; (f) you deleted your Account before we completed our review, as deletion permanently removes the records needed to verify the daily practice requirement; or (g) the Subscription was obtained or used in breach of our terms, or the request forms part of a pattern of repeated purchases and refunds, including by using multiple Accounts, Google accounts or payment methods, that abuses the 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee; or (h) the Payment for which a refund is requested is a renewal or any other Payment that is not the First Payment of the Subscription.

7.2 Where we reasonably believe that section 7.1(d) or 7.1(g) applies, we may refuse the request and may suspend the Account concerned, without prejudice to any other right we have. A Payment can be refunded only once.

08Cancellation, third-party refunds and statutory rights

8.1 You may cancel your Subscription at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. On cancellation, you will retain access to Premium features until the end of the period for which you have already paid and will not be charged again. Cancellation does not of itself entitle you to a refund of any amount already paid, save as provided in this Policy or required by law.

8.2 Google Play operates its own refund policy for purchases made through it, under which you may in certain circumstances request a refund directly from Google. Any refund granted by Google is governed by Google's policy, and a Payment refunded by Google cannot be refunded again under this Policy.

8.3 Nothing in this Policy excludes or limits any right or remedy you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you, including, in India, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the rules made under it. Where such law gives you a right to a refund in circumstances not described in this Policy, that right is unaffected.

09Amendments

9.1 We may amend this Policy from time to time. The amended Policy takes effect from the effective date shown at the top of this page. An amendment will not reduce the rights that applied to a Subscription when it was purchased, and each Subscription remains governed by the version of this Policy in force when it was purchased.

10General

10.1 If any provision of this Policy is held to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. Our failure to enforce any provision of this Policy shall not constitute a waiver of that provision. This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle the data, including Watch Time, used to verify the daily practice requirement.

11Contact

11.1 Refund requests may be made in the Application through Contact support or by email under section 4.2. Any question about this Policy should be sent through Contact support or to anvelopers368@gmail.com.
