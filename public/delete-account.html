Delete your Gomu account

You can permanently delete your Gomu account and all of its data at any time — from inside the app, or without the app by emailing us. This applies to Gomu on iPhone and Android, and to accounts created with email or with Google.

Option 1 · In the app (instant)

Delete it yourself in Gomu

  1. Open Gomu and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and scroll down to Danger zone.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm twice.

Your account and data are deleted immediately, and you're signed out. If you signed in with Google, Gomu is also disconnected from your Google account.

Option 2 · Without the app

Ask us to delete it

No longer have the app installed, or can't sign in? Email us and we'll delete your account for you.

Email a deletion request

Send it from the email address on your Gomu account (for Google sign-in, your Google email) with the subject "Delete my Gomu account".

anvelopers368@gmail.com

To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from the account's own email address, and we may reply to confirm before deleting. We complete the deletion within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your login — email and password, or your Google sign-in link
  • Your profile and onboarding answers — JLPT level, goals, daily target
  • Saved words and their full review history
  • Watch history, daily activity and streaks, and achievements
  • Your settings and any feedback you sent from the app

What we keep, and for how long

We don't keep any of your account data after deletion. Copies that may remain in our hosting provider's routine database backups expire and are permanently removed within 30 days.

Deleting your Gomu account doesn't affect your Google account. Video playback is handled by YouTube under Google's Privacy Policy; Gomu doesn't hold that data. See our Privacy Policy for everything we collect.
